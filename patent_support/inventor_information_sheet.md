## Instructions for Completion

1. **Fill out all sections completely** - Missing information delays filing

3. **List ALL public disclosures** - Even informal ones (blog posts, tweets)

5. **Gather supporting documentation** - Git logs, emails, notebooks
6. **Return to attorney within 3 business days** - Time is critical

**Questions?** Contact your patent attorney before the initial consultation.
