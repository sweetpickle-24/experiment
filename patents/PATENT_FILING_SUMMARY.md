**Q: Can I still publish my research?**

**Q: What if I can't afford $185K-370K?**  
A: Start with Option C ($62K-124K) for core patent only. Add others later via continuation. Or seek investor funding.

**Q: What if someone else files a similar patent?**  
A: Whoever files first gets priority. That's why filing within 30 days is critical. Your filing date establishes your rights.

**Q: Can I do this without an attorney?**

**Q: Should I focus on licensing or starting a company?**  
A: Depends on your goals. Licensing is passive income, lower risk. Startup is higher risk, potentially higher reward.
