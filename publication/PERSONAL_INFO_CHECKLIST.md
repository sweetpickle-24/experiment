## 💡 TIPS

1. **ORCID**: Get this first - it's free and takes 2 minutes at https://orcid.org/
2. **Email**: Use a permanent email (not institutional if you might leave)
3. **Affiliation**: Use official institution name (check their website)
4. **Patents**: Be transparent - it won't hurt your submission
5. **Co-authors**: Get their approval in writing before submitting
